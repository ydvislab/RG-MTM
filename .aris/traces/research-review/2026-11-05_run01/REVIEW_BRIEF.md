## What the paper claims (scope agreed with the co-author)
Position–topology only: (1) merge tree maps written as one constrained layout problem (Eq. general; Table 1), with TMTM and ST-MTM as exact-topology instances; (2) three certified bounds (Theorems order, width, chain) plus optimal barriers; (3) AnchorMTM with a fixed spatial reference and one control ε (Exact Mode ε=0 with numerical allowance ε0=1e-7; Budgeted Mode); (4) evaluation on seven sequences (917 frames). - Exact Mode on Tangaroa is 0.42 pp above ν* (width-aware search trades NROE for worst displacement).
