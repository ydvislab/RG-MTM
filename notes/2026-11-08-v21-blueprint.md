## 一、一句话主线

> 把合并树地图的轴锚定到空间，位置上必然要付出代价；我们算出任何地图都逃不掉的这份代价，做出一个只比它多付一点的地图，并用它说明现有地图的大部分误差并不是这份代价。

英文（摘要/结论用）：*Anchoring the axis of a merge tree map to space has an unavoidable positional price. We compute this price for any map, build maps that pay little more than it, and show that most of the error of existing maps is not this price.*

## 三、逐节结构与篇幅（9 页 = 18 栏）

### 标题与摘要（含首页图，约 1.2 栏）
- 标题不变：*AnchorMTM: Spatially Anchored Merge Tree Maps with Position Bounds*。
- 摘要按五步重写，草稿：

> Temporal merge tree maps show how the topology of a scalar field evolves in one image, but their display axis has no fixed spatial meaning, so a feature's position cannot be read as its location. Anchoring the axis to a spatial direction conflicts with the merge tree: every subtree must occupy one contiguous block, while the projected locations of sibling subtrees can interleave. We ask how much displacement this conflict forces. Three lower bounds answer this question for exact topology, finite feature widths and a bounded merge error. They depend only on the tree, the projected locations and the widths, so they measure any map and split its error into a part forced by the tree and a part chosen by its layout. AnchorMTM builds maps that approach the bounds, with a single control, a merge-error budget. The bounds equal the exhaustive optimum on controlled trees and are attained on 606 of 614 planar frames, and 31–95% of the order error of TMTM and ST-MTM is not forced by the tree. On the planar ST-MTM benchmark, AnchorMTM lowers the mean worst displacement by 73–98% within every declared budget, while ST-MTM preserves neighborhoods better.

### 1 引言（约 1.3 栏）
- P1 合并树地图做什么；一张总览要回答三件事：有哪些特征、在哪、怎么动；第二根轴的含义决定能回答哪些。（沿用 v20 P1）
- P2 TMTM/ST-MTM 的轴没有固定空间含义；Hovmöller 保留坐标但丢了层次。**缺口：没有同时保住两者的地图。**（沿用 P2，删减）
- P3 锚定 → 冲突（A,C,D,B 例，冲突示意图引用）；ST-MTM 指出一维布局必然失真，但多少、在哪、放松能换回多少没人量过。（沿用 P3）
- P4 我们的做法，按"先算代价、再逼近、再验证"的顺序写：
  *We first ask how much displacement the tree forces. … Because the bounds need no layout, they serve as a yardstick for any map … We then build AnchorMTM to approach them …*
- 贡献三条（与 §4、§5、§6 一一对应）：
  1. 问题表述 + 三个与布局无关的下界，可把任意地图的误差分为树强加的和布局选择的；
  2. AnchorMTM：一个旋钮 ε 的锚定构造，逼近下界；
  3. 评估：下界紧；现有地图 31–95% 的顺序误差可避免；AnchorMTM 在守预算的前提下位置误差低 73–98%；逐帧看预算买到什么。
