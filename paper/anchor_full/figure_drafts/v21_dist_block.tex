Figure~\ref{fig:budget-distribution} shows what the budget buys frame by frame. On the planar inputs it shortens the upper tail rather than shifting every frame. On Storms the median per-frame displacement moves only from 1.10\% to 0.84\% at $\varepsilon=10\%$, while the 90th percentile falls from 10.7\% to 2.5\% and the worst frame from 44.8\% to 11.5\%; 482 of the 744 frames keep zero merge error, and those that use the allowance form a few runs of consecutive frames (Supplementary Fig.~S2). On Wildfires the same 7 of 32 frames use the allowance at every budget, and on Ring the median falls from 3.9\% to 1.0\% while the last frame stays at 10.0\% until the budget reaches 63\%. In the volumes almost every frame contains a releasable conflict (200 of 201 Tangaroa and 98 of 102 Cylinder frames use the allowance), so the whole distribution moves: the Cylinder median falls from 6.8\% to 1.0\% already at 2\%, and the Tangaroa median from 11.3\% to 1.2\% at 20\%.
