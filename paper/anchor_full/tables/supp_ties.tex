At $\varepsilon_0=10^{-7}$, flattening every parent gap of at most $2\varepsilon_0\Delta_F$ and applying Theorem~\ref{thm:order} gives 7.72\%, the value reported in earlier drafts. It is valid but not attained: on frames 58 and 62, chains of near-tied saddles make it certify 4.85\% and 4.48\%, but the order that reaches these values has merge error 1.6 and 2.0 times $\varepsilon_0\Delta_F$. Enumerating all 92{,}160 and 368{,}640 orders that are legal after this flattening and keeping those within the allowance gives the optimum 19.03\% and 19.40\%, equal to $C_\eta$ of Theorem~\ref{thm:chain} at $\eta=\varepsilon_0\Delta_F$.
